# Episode 0 — Introduction: The Seven Pillars

*Target length: 60–90 s · Series: The Seven Pillars (StayTheWay)*

**[ON SCREEN: "The Seven Pillars — free & low-cost foundations"]**

Hi, and welcome. If you or someone you love is facing cancer or an autoimmune illness, it's easy to feel like everything that helps is expensive or complicated. It isn't always. This series walks through seven foundations most people can start this week, for little or no money.

**[ON SCREEN: list of 7 pillars]**

Whole-food nutrition. Clean water. Sleep. Movement. Core nutrients. Spirit — prayer, gratitude and faith. And connection with healthy boundaries.

**[ON SCREEN: "Strong · Observational · No effect found"]**

For each one we'll tell you honestly how strong the research is. Some pillars, like movement, have real trial evidence. Others are mostly observational. And a few popular ideas have been tested and didn't pan out — we'll tell you that too.

This is education, not medical advice. Talk with your own care team before changing anything, especially supplements. These pillars work alongside your treatment, never instead of it.
