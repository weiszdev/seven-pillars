# Episode 4 — Movement

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (StayTheWay)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 4 of 7 — Movement"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. If there's one pillar with real trial evidence for living longer after cancer, it's this one. Walking counts. Build toward regular aerobic activity plus some strength work, at a pace your team approves.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Start with a 10-minute walk after a meal; add a few minutes each week.
- Body-weight strength: sit-to-stands from a chair, wall push-ups.
- Walk with a friend or church group — it doubles as Pillar 7.
- Ask your team what's safe for you, especially during treatment.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Phase 3 RCT — strongest evidence in this series"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Phase 3 RCT)* CHALLENGE phase 3 randomized trial, 889 colon cancer survivors after chemo: a 3-year coached exercise program, disease-free survival HR 0.72 (0.55–0.94) and overall survival HR 0.63 (0.43–0.94) at ~8 years. [PMID 40450658]

What it doesn't prove: This was colon cancer with a coach. Muscle and joint side effects were more common in the exercise group (about 19% vs 12%). Start slowly. Autoimmune outcomes not gathered here.
