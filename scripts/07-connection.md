# Episode 7 — Connection & Healthy Boundaries

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (StayTheWay)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 7 of 7 — Connection & Healthy Boundaries"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. We were made for each other. Fellowship, a support group, and wise distance from relationships that consistently harm you.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Join a church small group or a hospital / cancer-center support group.
- Cancer Support Community offers free programs.
- Schedule one call or visit a week with someone who lifts you up.
- For hard relationships, talk with a pastor or counselor before making big changes.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Large meta-analysis of cohorts; survival RCT null, quality-of-life benefit"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Observational meta-analysis)* Meta-analysis of 148 studies, 308,849 people: stronger social relationships, 50% higher odds of survival (OR 1.50, 1.42–1.59). [PMID 20668659]
- *(RCT — null for survival)* Randomized trial, 235 women with metastatic breast cancer: group support did not extend survival (HR 1.23, 0.88–1.72) but improved mood and pain, most in distressed women. [PMID 11742045]

What it doesn't prove: Autoimmune outcomes not gathered here. Boundaries are about safety and peace, not cutting people off lightly.
