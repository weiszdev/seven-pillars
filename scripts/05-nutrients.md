# Episode 5 — Core Nutrient Sufficiency

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 5 of 7 — Core Nutrient Sufficiency"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. This is the pillar where 'more' is not always better. Fill measured gaps rather than taking everything. Test first, then supplement with your team's approval.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Low–Moderate"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Ask for a vitamin D blood test before choosing a dose.
- Fish twice a week covers omega-3 for many people.
- Fermented foods (yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut) are a low-cost probiotic option.
- Frozen vegetables and fruit instead of greens powders.
- A store-brand multivitamin if your diet has gaps.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Large RCT (vitamin D, omega-3); others not gathered"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Large RCT)* VITAL RCT, 25,871 adults 50+: vitamin D 2,000 IU/day reduced confirmed autoimmune disease (HR 0.78); omega-3 HR 0.85, not significant. [PMID 35082139]
- *(Large RCT)* VITAL: vitamin D did not reduce cancer cases (HR 0.96). Cancer deaths HR 0.83 (0.67–1.02), 0.75 after excluding the first two years (secondary analysis). [PMID 30415629]
- *(Observational, within a trial)* SWOG S0221: antioxidant supplement use during breast-cancer chemotherapy was associated with higher recurrence risk. [PMID 31855498]

**[ON SCREEN: table — Fab 5 vs generic options]**

Some of you asked about NutriDyn's "Fab 5." Those are a commercial practitioner brand. For each one there's a simpler, often cheaper option: D3 5000 with K2 — or store-brand d3, dose from your blood test; Omega Pure EPA-DHA 720 — or fish twice a week or store-brand fish oil; UltraBiotic Daily Multi-Strain — or yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut; Fruits & Greens — or frozen vegetables and fruit; Essential Multi — or store-brand multivitamin.

**[ON SCREEN: "Experiential evidence — Radical Remission"]**

One more lens, kept separate from the trials: in Dr. Kelly Turner's study of 1,000+ radical remissions, people often reported "using herbs and supplements". That's experience, not proof — inspiring, but with no comparison group it can't show cause.

What it doesn't prove: 5,000 IU vitamin D is well above the 2,000 IU tested in VITAL, and people whose levels are already high may not need more. Probiotics may be a concern when immunity is very low. Supplements can interact with treatment.
