# Episode 1 — Whole-Food Nutrition

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 1 of 7 — Whole-Food Nutrition"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. Food is one of the most hopeful places to start, because you make choices about it every day. Mostly plants, beans, whole grains, fish, olive oil and nuts; fewer ultra-processed foods. Organic where the budget allows.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Low"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Cook dried beans and lentils in bulk; use frozen vegetables and fruit (just as nutritious, cheaper, less waste).
- Oats, eggs, canned sardines or salmon, seasonal produce.
- If buying organic, prioritize the produce you eat most; wash all produce.
- Cut back on sugary drinks and packaged, ultra-processed snacks.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — One small RCT subgroup + observational cohorts"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Small RCT subgroup)* PREDIMED randomized trial subgroup: Mediterranean diet + extra-virgin olive oil, invasive breast cancer HR 0.32 (0.13–0.79). Only 35 cases; a secondary outcome. [PMID 26365989]
- *(Observational)* NutriNet-Santé cohort, 68,946 adults: highest vs lowest organic-food score, cancer HR 0.75 (0.63–0.88). Absolute difference small; organic buyers tend to be healthier overall. [PMID 30422212]

What it doesn't prove: No strong autoimmune outcome trials were gathered here. The organic finding may partly reflect lifestyle, income and activity.
