# Episode 3 — Sleep & Daily Rhythm

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 3 of 7 — Sleep & Daily Rhythm"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. Sleep is where the body does its repair work — and it's free. Consistent, protected sleep: same wake time every day, morning daylight, a dark cool room, screens off before bed.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Same wake-up time seven days a week.
- Ten minutes of outdoor morning light.
- Dark, cool, quiet bedroom; phone out of reach.
- Free app: CBT-i Coach from the US Department of Veterans Affairs.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — RCT meta-analysis (sleep & symptom outcomes)"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(RCT meta-analysis)* Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs, 752 cancer survivors: CBT for insomnia improved insomnia severity (d = 0.77) and sleep efficiency (d = 0.53), lasting up to 6 months. [PMID 26434673]

What it doesn't prove: Better sleep improves how people feel and function; trials have not shown it changes cancer recurrence. Autoimmune outcome trials not gathered here.
