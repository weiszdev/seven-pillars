# Episode 2 — Clean Water & Fewer Toxins

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 2 of 7 — Clean Water & Fewer Toxins"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. Water is simple, but the honest answer is: it depends on what's in yours. Know what is in your water, then filter for that specific thing with a certified filter.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free–Low"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Free: read your utility's annual Consumer Confidence Report (water quality report).
- Well owners: many state health departments offer low-cost testing.
- Low cost: a pitcher or faucet filter certified to the NSF/ANSI standard for your contaminant (e.g., lead, PFAS).
- If pipes may contain lead, run cold water before drinking; never use hot tap water for cooking.
- Free: ventilate, fix moisture and mold, avoid heating food in plastic.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Exposure-specific (no outcome trials of filtering safe water)"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Regulatory / exposure science)* Lead, arsenic, nitrate and some PFAS have documented harms at certain levels (EPA drinking-water standards).

What it doesn't prove: There is no trial showing that filtering already-safe water improves cancer or autoimmune outcomes, so we give no effect size here on purpose.
