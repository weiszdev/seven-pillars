# Episode 6 — Spirit: Prayer, Gratitude & Faith

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 6 of 7 — Spirit: Prayer, Gratitude & Faith"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. For many of us, faith isn't an add-on. It's the foundation the rest stands on. Personal prayer, Scripture, worship, and a daily gratitude practice.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Write down three things you're thankful for each night.
- A set daily time for prayer and Scripture.
- Gather for worship with a local church.
- Let others pray with you, in person.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Observational for personal practice; RCT null for distant intercessory prayer"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Observational)* Nurses' Health Study, 74,534 women: attending religious services more than weekly vs never, death HR 0.67 (0.62–0.71). [PMID 27183175]
- *(RCT — null)* STEP randomized trial, 1,802 bypass patients: prayer by strangers did not reduce complications; patients told they were being prayed for had slightly more. [PMID 16569567]
- *(Small RCTs)* Gratitude-practice trials generally show small improvements in well-being (pooled effect not verified here).

**[ON SCREEN: "Experiential evidence — Radical Remission"]**

One more lens, kept separate from the trials: in Dr. Kelly Turner's study of 1,000+ radical remissions, people often reported "deepening your spiritual connection", "increasing positive emotions", "following your intuition". That's experience, not proof — inspiring, but with no comparison group it can't show cause.

Cancer survivor Chris Wark also often credits his faith and prayer as part of his journey. That's a personal story, not a study, but it may encourage you.

What it doesn't prove: The benefit seen with attendance may come partly through community, support and healthier habits. Faith is worth practicing for its own sake, without needing a hazard ratio.
