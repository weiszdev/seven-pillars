Spirit: Prayer, Gratitude & Faith

Personal prayer, Scripture, worship, and a daily gratitude practice.

Cost: Free · Observational for personal practice; RCT null for distant intercessory prayer

▶ Video placeholder — Episode 6: Spirit: Prayer, Gratitude & Faith
Script: scripts.md (episode 6)

Free & low-cost actions

  • Write down three things you're thankful for each night.
  • A set daily time for prayer and Scripture.
  • Gather for worship with a local church.
  • Let others pray with you, in person.

What the research says

  • Observational
    Nurses' Health Study, 74,534 women: attending religious services more than weekly vs never, death HR 0.67 (0.62–0.71). PubMed 27183175
  • RCT — null
    STEP randomized trial, 1,802 bypass patients: prayer by strangers did not reduce complications; patients told they were being prayed for had slightly more. PubMed 16569567
  • Small RCTs
    Gratitude-practice trials generally show small improvements in well-being (pooled effect not verified here).

Honest limits

The benefit seen with attendance may come partly through community, support and healthier habits. Faith is worth practicing for its own sake, without needing a hazard ratio.
