Faith foundation

Each of the seven pillars has roots in Scripture. Below are a few verses for each pillar, quoted from the King James Version (public domain), with a short note on why each one fits. Some fit directly. Others are spiritual imagery, and the notes say which.

BENR: Body, Emotions, Neuro, Repair

Each pillar is matched to the part of the BENR framework it serves. BENR is a biblical tool for reflecting on whole-person healing. It is not therapy or diagnosis.

🌿 Whole-food nutrition

BENR: B · Body

"And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat." Genesis 1:29 (KJV)
"Prove thy servants, I beseech thee, ten days; and let them give us pulse to eat, and water to drink." Daniel 1:12 (KJV)
"Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God." 1 Corinthians 10:31 (KJV)

Why: Scripture begins with plants given as food, and Daniel chose simple food over the king's rich table. Eating is treated as stewardship, done to the glory of God.

💧 Clean water

BENR: B · Body; R · Repair

"He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters." Psalm 23:2 (KJV)
"And the LORD shall guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drought, and make fat thy bones: and thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a spring of water, whose waters fail not." Isaiah 58:11 (KJV)

Why: Still waters and a well-watered garden are pictures of restoration. These verses are mainly spiritual imagery, not instructions about filtering water, and we include them as imagery.

🌙 Restful sleep

BENR: B · Body; N · Neuro; R · Repair

"It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep." Psalm 127:2 (KJV)
"I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety." Psalm 4:8 (KJV)
"And he said unto them, Come ye yourselves apart into a desert place, and rest a while: for there were many coming and going, and they had no leisure so much as to eat." Mark 6:31 (KJV)

Why: Sleep is described as a gift, and Jesus called his disciples aside to rest. Rest is an act of trust, not laziness.

🥾 Daily movement

BENR: B · Body

"For bodily exercise profiteth little: but godliness is profitable unto all things, having promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come." 1 Timothy 4:8 (KJV)
"What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God’s." 1 Corinthians 6:19-20 (KJV)
"But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint." Isaiah 40:31 (KJV)

Why: Paul says bodily exercise "profiteth little" next to godliness. That is a modest benefit, but a real one. The body is a temple to steward, and strength is renewed in waiting on the Lord.

🌱 Core nutrients

BENR: B · Body; R · Repair

"Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth." 3 John 1:2 (KJV)
"And by the river upon the bank thereof, on this side and on that side, shall grow all trees for meat, whose leaf shall not fade, neither shall the fruit thereof be consumed: it shall bring forth new fruit according to his months, because their waters they issued out of the sanctuary: and the fruit thereof shall be for meat, and the leaf thereof for medicine." Ezekiel 47:12 (KJV)

Why: John prays for health of both body and soul. Ezekiel's vision pictures fruit for food and leaves "for medicine," which affirms that creation provides for healing.

🌅 Prayer & gratefulness

BENR: E · Emotions; N · Neuro

"Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus." Philippians 4:6-7 (KJV)
"Rejoice evermore. Pray without ceasing. In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you." 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18 (KJV)
"A merry heart doeth good like a medicine: but a broken spirit drieth the bones." Proverbs 17:22 (KJV)

Why: Prayer with thanksgiving is paired with peace that "shall keep your hearts and minds." A merry heart is called good medicine.

🤝 Fellowship, support & healthy boundaries

BENR: E · Emotions; R · Repair

"And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works: Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching." Hebrews 10:24-25 (KJV)
"Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour. For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up." Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 (KJV)
"He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed." Proverbs 13:20 (KJV)
"Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners." 1 Corinthians 15:33 (KJV)

Why: We are called to gather and lift one another up. Scripture also warns that harmful company corrupts, which supports setting wise boundaries.
