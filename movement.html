PILLAR 4 OF 7

Movement

Walking counts. Build toward regular aerobic activity plus some strength work, at a pace your team approves.

Cost: Free · Phase 3 RCT — strongest evidence in this series

▶ Video placeholder — Episode 4: Movement
Script: scripts.md (episode 4)

Free & low-cost actions

  • Start with a 10-minute walk after a meal; add a few minutes each week.
  • Body-weight strength: sit-to-stands from a chair, wall push-ups.
  • Walk with a friend or church group — it doubles as Pillar 7.
  • Ask your team what's safe for you, especially during treatment.

What the research says

  • Phase 3 RCT
    CHALLENGE phase 3 randomized trial, 889 colon cancer survivors after chemo: a 3-year coached exercise program, disease-free survival HR 0.72 (0.55–0.94) and overall survival HR 0.63 (0.43–0.94) at ~8 years. PubMed 40450658

Honest limits

This was colon cancer with a coach. Muscle and joint side effects were more common in the exercise group (about 19% vs 12%). Start slowly. Autoimmune outcomes not gathered here.
