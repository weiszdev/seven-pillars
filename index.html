How to read the evidence grades

  • Randomized trial (RCT): strongest; the practice was tested directly.
  • Observational: a link was seen in large groups, but other factors may explain it.
  • Null: tested and no effect on that outcome was found — we report these too.
  • Experiential / case-based: patterns reported by people who experienced remission; valuable for hypotheses and hope, but no comparison group, so it can't show cause or how often it works.

Where the evidence is strongest

Ranked by strength of evidence for cancer and survival outcomes, not by personal importance.

  1. Movement: Randomized trial: in CHALLENGE, a coached exercise program cut deaths among colon cancer survivors (overall survival HR 0.63). PubMed 40450658
  2. Connection: Observational: stronger social ties linked to 50% higher odds of survival (Holt-Lunstad meta-analysis, all-cause, general populations). PubMed 20668659 Support-group trials improved quality of life, not survival.
  3. Whole-food nutrition: Consistent observational links plus one small trial subgroup; organic food is the weakest part.
  4. Spirit: Observational: attending religious services more than weekly linked to lower death rates (HR 0.67, all-cause). PubMed 27183175
  5. Sleep: Observational links; CBT for insomnia trials improve sleep and quality of life in survivors. PubMed 26434673
  6. Core nutrients: Fix true deficiencies. Vitamin D (VITAL) didn't reduce cancer cases; there was a possible signal for fewer cancer deaths. PubMed 30415629 Antioxidant supplements during chemo were linked to more recurrence. PubMed 31855498
  7. Clean water: Exposure-specific: no outcome trials of filtering already-safe water. Test your water first.

Your personal order can shift: fix your biggest gap first. These pillars complement care from your treatment team, never replace it.

🌿PILLAR 1

Whole-Food Nutrition

Mostly plants, beans, whole grains, fish, olive oil and nuts; fewer ultra-processed foods. Organic where the budget allows.

💧PILLAR 2

Clean Water & Fewer Toxins

Know what is in your water, then filter for that specific thing with a certified filter.

🌙PILLAR 3

Sleep & Daily Rhythm

Consistent, protected sleep: same wake time every day, morning daylight, a dark cool room, screens off before bed.

🥾PILLAR 4

Movement

Walking counts. Build toward regular aerobic activity plus some strength work, at a pace your team approves.

🌱PILLAR 5

Core Nutrient Sufficiency

Fill measured gaps rather than taking everything. Test first, then supplement with your team's approval.

🌅PILLAR 6

Spirit: Prayer, Gratitude & Faith

Personal prayer, Scripture, worship, and a daily gratitude practice.

🤝PILLAR 7

Connection & Healthy Boundaries

Fellowship, a support group, and wise distance from relationships that consistently harm you.
