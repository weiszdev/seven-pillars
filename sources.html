Sources

  • PREDIMED randomized trial subgroup: Mediterranean diet + extra-virgin olive oil, invasive breast cancer HR 0.32 (0.13–0.79). Only 35 cases; a secondary outcome. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26365989/
  • NutriNet-Santé cohort, 68,946 adults: highest vs lowest organic-food score, cancer HR 0.75 (0.63–0.88). Absolute difference small; organic buyers tend to be healthier overall. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30422212/
  • Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs, 752 cancer survivors: CBT for insomnia improved insomnia severity (d = 0.77) and sleep efficiency (d = 0.53), lasting up to 6 months. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26434673/
  • CHALLENGE phase 3 randomized trial, 889 colon cancer survivors after chemo: a 3-year coached exercise program, disease-free survival HR 0.72 (0.55–0.94) and overall survival HR 0.63 (0.43–0.94) at ~8 years. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40450658/
  • VITAL RCT, 25,871 adults 50+: vitamin D 2,000 IU/day reduced confirmed autoimmune disease (HR 0.78); omega-3 HR 0.85, not significant. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35082139/
  • VITAL: vitamin D did not reduce cancer cases (HR 0.96). Cancer deaths HR 0.83 (0.67–1.02), 0.75 after excluding the first two years (secondary analysis). — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30415629/
  • SWOG S0221: antioxidant supplement use during breast-cancer chemotherapy was associated with higher recurrence risk. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31855498/
  • Nurses' Health Study, 74,534 women: attending religious services more than weekly vs never, death HR 0.67 (0.62–0.71). — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27183175/
  • STEP randomized trial, 1,802 bypass patients: prayer by strangers did not reduce complications; patients told they were being prayed for had slightly more. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16569567/
  • Meta-analysis of 148 studies, 308,849 people: stronger social relationships, 50% higher odds of survival (OR 1.50, 1.42–1.59). — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20668659/
  • Randomized trial, 235 women with metastatic breast cancer: group support did not extend survival (HR 1.23, 0.88–1.72) but improved mood and pain, most in distressed women. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11742045/
