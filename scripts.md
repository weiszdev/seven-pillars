# Episode 1 — Whole-Food Nutrition

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 1 of 7 — Whole-Food Nutrition"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. Food is one of the most hopeful places to start, because you make choices about it every day. Mostly plants, beans, whole grains, fish, olive oil and nuts; fewer ultra-processed foods. Organic where the budget allows.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Low"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Cook dried beans and lentils in bulk; use frozen vegetables and fruit (just as nutritious, cheaper, less waste).
- Oats, eggs, canned sardines or salmon, seasonal produce.
- If buying organic, prioritize the produce you eat most; wash all produce.
- Cut back on sugary drinks and packaged, ultra-processed snacks.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — One small RCT subgroup + observational cohorts"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Small RCT subgroup)* PREDIMED randomized trial subgroup: Mediterranean diet + extra-virgin olive oil, invasive breast cancer HR 0.32 (0.13–0.79). Only 35 cases; a secondary outcome. [PMID 26365989]
- *(Observational)* NutriNet-Santé cohort, 68,946 adults: highest vs lowest organic-food score, cancer HR 0.75 (0.63–0.88). Absolute difference small; organic buyers tend to be healthier overall. [PMID 30422212]

**[ON SCREEN: "Experiential evidence — Radical Remission"]**

One more lens, kept separate from the trials: in Dr. Kelly Turner's study of 1,000+ radical remissions, people often reported "radically changing your diet". That's experience, not proof — inspiring, but with no comparison group it can't show cause.

What it doesn't prove: No strong autoimmune outcome trials were gathered here. The organic finding may partly reflect lifestyle, income and activity.

# Episode 2 — Clean Water & Fewer Toxins

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 2 of 7 — Clean Water & Fewer Toxins"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. Water is simple, but the honest answer is: it depends on what's in yours. Know what is in your water, then filter for that specific thing with a certified filter.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free–Low"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Free: read your utility's annual Consumer Confidence Report (water quality report).
- Well owners: many state health departments offer low-cost testing.
- Low cost: a pitcher or faucet filter certified to the NSF/ANSI standard for your contaminant (e.g., lead, PFAS).
- If pipes may contain lead, run cold water before drinking; never use hot tap water for cooking.
- Free: ventilate, fix moisture and mold, avoid heating food in plastic.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Exposure-specific (no outcome trials of filtering safe water)"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Regulatory / exposure science)* Lead, arsenic, nitrate and some PFAS have documented harms at certain levels (EPA drinking-water standards).

What it doesn't prove: There is no trial showing that filtering already-safe water improves cancer or autoimmune outcomes, so we give no effect size here on purpose.

# Episode 3 — Sleep & Daily Rhythm

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 3 of 7 — Sleep & Daily Rhythm"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. Sleep is where the body does its repair work — and it's free. Consistent, protected sleep: same wake time every day, morning daylight, a dark cool room, screens off before bed.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Same wake-up time seven days a week.
- Ten minutes of outdoor morning light.
- Dark, cool, quiet bedroom; phone out of reach.
- Free app: CBT-i Coach from the US Department of Veterans Affairs.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — RCT meta-analysis (sleep & symptom outcomes)"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(RCT meta-analysis)* Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs, 752 cancer survivors: CBT for insomnia improved insomnia severity (d = 0.77) and sleep efficiency (d = 0.53), lasting up to 6 months. [PMID 26434673]

What it doesn't prove: Better sleep improves how people feel and function; trials have not shown it changes cancer recurrence. Autoimmune outcome trials not gathered here.

# Episode 4 — Movement

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 4 of 7 — Movement"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. If there's one pillar with real trial evidence for living longer after cancer, it's this one. Walking counts. Build toward regular aerobic activity plus some strength work, at a pace your team approves.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Start with a 10-minute walk after a meal; add a few minutes each week.
- Body-weight strength: sit-to-stands from a chair, wall push-ups.
- Walk with a friend or church group — it doubles as Pillar 7.
- Ask your team what's safe for you, especially during treatment.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Phase 3 RCT — strongest evidence in this series"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Phase 3 RCT)* CHALLENGE phase 3 randomized trial, 889 colon cancer survivors after chemo: a 3-year coached exercise program, disease-free survival HR 0.72 (0.55–0.94) and overall survival HR 0.63 (0.43–0.94) at ~8 years. [PMID 40450658]

**[ON SCREEN: "Experiential evidence — Radical Remission"]**

One more lens, kept separate from the trials: in Dr. Kelly Turner's study of 1,000+ radical remissions, people often reported "exercise and movement (added as the 10th factor in radical hope, 2020)". That's experience, not proof — inspiring, but with no comparison group it can't show cause.

What it doesn't prove: This was colon cancer with a coach. Muscle and joint side effects were more common in the exercise group (about 19% vs 12%). Start slowly. Autoimmune outcomes not gathered here.

# Episode 5 — Core Nutrient Sufficiency

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 5 of 7 — Core Nutrient Sufficiency"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. This is the pillar where 'more' is not always better. Fill measured gaps rather than taking everything. Test first, then supplement with your team's approval.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Low–Moderate"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Ask for a vitamin D blood test before choosing a dose.
- Fish twice a week covers omega-3 for many people.
- Fermented foods (yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut) are a low-cost probiotic option.
- Frozen vegetables and fruit instead of greens powders.
- A store-brand multivitamin if your diet has gaps.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Large RCT (vitamin D, omega-3); others not gathered"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Large RCT)* VITAL RCT, 25,871 adults 50+: vitamin D 2,000 IU/day reduced confirmed autoimmune disease (HR 0.78); omega-3 HR 0.85, not significant. [PMID 35082139]
- *(Large RCT)* VITAL: vitamin D did not reduce cancer cases (HR 0.96). Cancer deaths HR 0.83 (0.67–1.02), 0.75 after excluding the first two years (secondary analysis). [PMID 30415629]
- *(Observational, within a trial)* SWOG S0221: antioxidant supplement use during breast-cancer chemotherapy was associated with higher recurrence risk. [PMID 31855498]

**[ON SCREEN: table — Fab 5 vs generic options]**

Some of you asked about NutriDyn's "Fab 5." Those are a commercial practitioner brand. For each one there's a simpler, often cheaper option: D3 5000 with K2 — or store-brand d3, dose from your blood test; Omega Pure EPA-DHA 720 — or fish twice a week or store-brand fish oil; UltraBiotic Daily Multi-Strain — or yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut; Fruits & Greens — or frozen vegetables and fruit; Essential Multi — or store-brand multivitamin.

**[ON SCREEN: "Experiential evidence — Radical Remission"]**

One more lens, kept separate from the trials: in Dr. Kelly Turner's study of 1,000+ radical remissions, people often reported "using herbs and supplements". That's experience, not proof — inspiring, but with no comparison group it can't show cause.

What it doesn't prove: 5,000 IU vitamin D is well above the 2,000 IU tested in VITAL, and people whose levels are already high may not need more. Probiotics may be a concern when immunity is very low. Supplements can interact with treatment.

# Episode 6 — Spirit: Prayer, Gratitude & Faith

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 6 of 7 — Spirit: Prayer, Gratitude & Faith"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. For many of us, faith isn't an add-on. It's the foundation the rest stands on. Personal prayer, Scripture, worship, and a daily gratitude practice.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Write down three things you're thankful for each night.
- A set daily time for prayer and Scripture.
- Gather for worship with a local church.
- Let others pray with you, in person.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Observational for personal practice; RCT null for distant intercessory prayer"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Observational)* Nurses' Health Study, 74,534 women: attending religious services more than weekly vs never, death HR 0.67 (0.62–0.71). [PMID 27183175]
- *(RCT — null)* STEP randomized trial, 1,802 bypass patients: prayer by strangers did not reduce complications; patients told they were being prayed for had slightly more. [PMID 16569567]
- *(Small RCTs)* Gratitude-practice trials generally show small improvements in well-being (pooled effect not verified here).

**[ON SCREEN: "Experiential evidence — Radical Remission"]**

One more lens, kept separate from the trials: in Dr. Kelly Turner's study of 1,000+ radical remissions, people often reported "deepening your spiritual connection", "increasing positive emotions", "following your intuition". That's experience, not proof — inspiring, but with no comparison group it can't show cause.

What it doesn't prove: The benefit seen with attendance may come partly through community, support and healthier habits. Faith is worth practicing for its own sake, without needing a hazard ratio.

# Episode 7 — Connection & Healthy Boundaries

*Target length: 2–3 min · Series: The Seven Pillars (NotDrWise)*

**[ON SCREEN: "Pillar 7 of 7 — Connection & Healthy Boundaries"]**

Hey friends, welcome back to the Seven Pillars. We were made for each other. Fellowship, a support group, and wise distance from relationships that consistently harm you.

**[ON SCREEN: "Cost: Free"]**

Here are a few simple, low-cost ways to start:

- Join a church small group or a hospital / cancer-center support group.
- Cancer Support Community offers free programs.
- Schedule one call or visit a week with someone who lifts you up.
- For hard relationships, talk with a pastor or counselor before making big changes.

**[ON SCREEN: "What the research says — Large meta-analysis of cohorts; survival RCT null, quality-of-life benefit"]**

Now, let's be honest about the evidence, because you deserve the truth, not hype.

- *(Observational meta-analysis)* Meta-analysis of 148 studies, 308,849 people: stronger social relationships, 50% higher odds of survival (OR 1.50, 1.42–1.59). [PMID 20668659]
- *(RCT — null for survival)* Randomized trial, 235 women with metastatic breast cancer: group support did not extend survival (HR 1.23, 0.88–1.72) but improved mood and pain, most in distressed women. [PMID 11742045]

**[ON SCREEN: "Experiential evidence — Radical Remission"]**

One more lens, kept separate from the trials: in Dr. Kelly Turner's study of 1,000+ radical remissions, people often reported "embracing social support", "releasing suppressed emotions", "having strong reasons for living", "taking control of your health". That's experience, not proof — inspiring, but with no comparison group it can't show cause.

What it doesn't prove: Autoimmune outcomes not gathered here. Boundaries are about safety and peace, not cutting people off lightly.
