Sleep & Daily Rhythm

Consistent, protected sleep: same wake time every day, morning daylight, a dark cool room, screens off before bed.

Cost: Free · RCT meta-analysis (sleep & symptom outcomes)

▶ Video placeholder — Episode 3: Sleep & Daily Rhythm
Script: scripts.md (episode 3)

Free & low-cost actions

  • Same wake-up time seven days a week.
  • Ten minutes of outdoor morning light.
  • Dark, cool, quiet bedroom; phone out of reach.
  • Free app: CBT-i Coach from the US Department of Veterans Affairs.

What the research says

  • RCT meta-analysis
    Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs, 752 cancer survivors: CBT for insomnia improved insomnia severity (d = 0.77) and sleep efficiency (d = 0.53), lasting up to 6 months. PubMed 26434673

Honest limits

Better sleep improves how people feel and function; trials have not shown it changes cancer recurrence. Autoimmune outcome trials not gathered here.
