Connection & Healthy Boundaries

Fellowship, a support group, and wise distance from relationships that consistently harm you.

Cost: Free · Large meta-analysis of cohorts; survival RCT null, quality-of-life benefit

Free & low-cost actions

  • Join a church small group or a hospital / cancer-center support group.
  • Cancer Support Community offers free programs.
  • Schedule one call or visit a week with someone who lifts you up.
  • For hard relationships, talk with a pastor or counselor before making big changes.

What the research says

  • Observational meta-analysis
    Meta-analysis of 148 studies, 308,849 people: stronger social relationships, 50% higher odds of survival (OR 1.50, 1.42–1.59). PubMed 20668659
  • RCT — null for survival
    Randomized trial, 235 women with metastatic breast cancer: group support did not extend survival (HR 1.23, 0.88–1.72) but improved mood and pain, most in distressed women. PubMed 11742045

Honest limits

Autoimmune outcomes not gathered here. Boundaries are about safety and peace, not cutting people off lightly.
